# VANTORA AI
### AI-Powered Business Consulting & Intelligence Platform

VANTORA AI is an AI-powered business consulting platform designed to help consultants structure client engagements, transform business challenges into actionable strategies, and manage the journey from diagnosis to optimization.

**Developed as part of VANTORA**, a business-solutions consultancy with a Lebanon-first, global-by-design vision.

## Overview

VANTORA AI is designed to support a structured consulting workflow, helping organize business information, research, strategic decisions, execution, and performance measurement in one platform.

## Core Capabilities

- **Business Diagnosis** — Structure client challenges and identify areas requiring investigation.
- **Business Research** — Organize research and supporting evidence for consulting decisions.
- **Opportunity Identification** — Explore potential business opportunities and growth areas.
- **Competitor Intelligence** — Support structured competitor analysis.
- **Financial Analysis** — Help organize and assess business financial information.
- **Strategic Planning** — Turn findings into actionable plans.
- **Execution Management** — Track project activities and progress.
- **Measurement & Optimization** — Monitor KPIs and identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Reporting** — Organize project findings and consulting deliverables.

## Consulting Workflow

**Diagnose → Research → Analyze → Identify Opportunities → Strategize → Plan → Execute → Measure → Optimize**
